### whoami

I work as a systems and cybersecurity technician at **ITBacking**, looking after endpoint security, monitoring and infrastructure for several clients. Day to day that means Wazuh, backups and recovery with Acronis, firewalls, networking and **ENS** compliance work (SIEM alert review, patching, asset inventory, capacity reviews).

I got into security through S4vitar's content, and now I'm going the offensive route: I'm working through the **Hack4u** courses to sit the **eJPT**, with pentesting as the next step.

I also co-founded **ÓRBITA Studios**, a small web agency, so I've been on the building side too.
